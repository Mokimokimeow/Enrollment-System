Enrollment Guide

Follow these simple steps to enroll at our university

Create Account

Register as a new student by clicking the Register button and filling in your personal information.

2

Complete Profile

Fill out your academic background, contact details, and upload required documents.

3

Select Courses

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues during the enrollment process, please contact our admissions office at admissions@university.edu or call +62 21 1234 5678.

Payment Instructions

Choose your preferred payment method to complete your enrollment

Online Payment

Log in to your student portal and navigate to the Payment section

Select your enrollment fees and choose your preferred payment method

Complete payment using credit card, bank transfer, or e-wallet

Receive instant confirmation via email

Accepted Methods: Visa, Mastercard, Bank Transfer, GoPay, OVO, Dana

Walk-in Payment

Visit the Finance Office at Main Building, 2nd Floor

Bring your student ID and enrollment invoice

Pay using cash, debit card, or bank transfer at the counter

Receive official receipt and payment confirmation

Office Hours: Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Important Notice

Please ensure payment is completed within 7 days of enrollment confirmation. Late payments may result in course cancellation. Keep your payment receipt for future reference.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Invest in your future with our financial support programs

Merit-Based Scholarships

Awarded to students with exceptional academic achievements, leadership qualities, and extracurricular involvement.

  • Up to 100% tuition coverage
  • Renewable for 4 years
  • GPA requirement: 3.5+

Need-Based Grants

Financial assistance for students who demonstrate financial need and meet academic requirements.

  • Based on family income
  • Up to 75% tuition coverage
  • Requires FAFSA documentation

Program-Specific Awards

Special scholarships for students enrolled in specific programs like STEM, Arts, or Business.

  • Varies by program
  • Research opportunities included
  • Mentorship programs

International Student Aid

Financial support for international students to help with tuition and living expenses.

  • Up to 50% tuition reduction
  • Housing assistance available
  • English language support

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information including name, email, phone number, date of birth, and address for enrollment purposes. We also collect academic records and documents submitted during the application process.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process applications, communicate about enrollment status, provide academic services, and improve our educational programs. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data is encrypted and stored securely.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

5. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact privacy@university.edu

Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using our enrollment system, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use our services.

2. Enrollment Requirements

Students must meet all admission requirements and provide accurate information. False information may result in rejection of application or dismissal from the university.

3. Academic Integrity

All students must maintain academic honesty. Cheating, plagiarism, or any form of academic dishonesty will result in disciplinary action.

4. Payment Obligations

Students are responsible for timely payment of tuition and fees. Late payments may incur penalties and affect enrollment status.

5. Code of Conduct

All students must adhere to the university's code of conduct both on and off campus. Violations may result in disciplinary action.

Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide better user experience, analyze site usage, and assist in our marketing efforts.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use essential cookies for site functionality, analytical cookies to understand user behavior, and marketing cookies to personalize your experience.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your behalf. These include analytics tools and social media integrations.

Accessibility Statement

1. Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

2. Accessibility Features

Our site includes features such as keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, color contrast compliance, and resizable text to accommodate users with different needs.

3. Technical Standards

We strive to follow WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines to ensure our content is accessible to all users regardless of their abilities.

4. Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility issues or have suggestions for improvement, please contact accessibility@university.edu
